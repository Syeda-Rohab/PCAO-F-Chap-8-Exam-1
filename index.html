Claude aur ChatGPT 101: Aik Crash Course

Chapter Explanation — Roman Urdu (Sabse Aasan Version)

Shuruaat

Intro:

Aap Claude ya ChatGPT kholte hain aur bohat saare buttons dikhte hain — model chunna, Plus menu, Projects, Memory, Skills, Connectors. Naam alag alag hain lekin asal cheez ek hi hai. Ye chapter Claude aur ChatGPT dono ko saath sikhata hai — buttons yaad karne ki bajaye, unke peeche wala tareeqa samajhna hai.

Zaroori baat:

Claude "artifact" bolta hai, ChatGPT "writing block" bolta hai. Claude "connector" bolta hai, ChatGPT "app" bolta hai. Naam alag hain, kaam wahi hai. Isi liye ye chapter aik nahin, do jagah sikhata hai.

Hissa 1: Cockpit

Claude aur ChatGPT — naam alag, kaam bilkul wahi

Idea 1 (Do jagah, ek tareeqa):

Claude aur ChatGPT dono ek jaisi cheezein dete hain: purani chats ki list, message box, model chunne ka control, files upload karna, Projects, memory, aur search. Dono jagah wahi hunar chalti hai: kaam saaf saaf batana, zaroori jaankari dena, decide karna kya AI ko dena hai, jawab check karna. Achi aadat: kisi bhi zaroori sawal se pehle 3 cheezein batao — aap kaun ho, kya chahiye, aur kaunse rules follow hon.

Idea 2 (Model chunna aur Thinking Mode):

Dono AI aapko chunne dete hain kitni "soch" (capability) is kaam par kharch karni hai. 3 levels: Fast (rozmarra kaam, tez aur sasta), Thinking (mushkil sawal, dheema lekin gehra), Heavy (sabse mushkil kaam, sabse dheema aur mehnga). Naam badalte rehte hain lekin ye 3 levels hamesha rehte hain. Rule 1: Fast se shuru karo. Rule 2: Kaam mushkil ho (kai steps, math, tricky code) to level barhao.

Idea 3 (Wo cheezein jo aap attach karte ho):

AI sirf wahi dekh sakta hai jo aap uske saamne rakhte ho. Files, PDF, spreadsheet, screenshot attach karna AI ko zyada context deta hai. "Is contract ka summary do" aur "is contract ka summary do (file attached)" — alfaz same hain, lekin doosra kaam ka hai kyun ke AI asal file dekh sakta hai. Achi aadat: apne aap se poocho "ek qabil colleague ko theek jawab dene se pehle kya dikhana zaroori hoga?" phir wahi AI ko do.

Hissa 2: Apna Workspace Banana

Idea 4 (Projects):

Kuch hafton baad aapki chat list ulajh jati hai — ek hi client ke baare mein 4 alag chats. Project ek kamra hai jahan ek kaam ki har cheez ek jagah rehti hai: chats, Knowledge (files), aur Instructions (hamesha lagu hone wale rules). Test: agar aap ne wahi background 3 baar samjhaya ya wahi file 3 baar upload ki, ab is kaam ka Project banana chahiye. Claude project files barhne par "search" karta hai (retrieval), ChatGPT project ke andar apni alag memory rakh sakta hai.

3 tarah ki yaadasht — teeno alag kaam ke liye hain

Idea 5 (Memory aur Instructions):

Standing Instructions = wo stable rules jo hamesha follow honi hain, jaise "hamesha seedha jawab do." Memory = AI ki apni chats se seekhi hui, badalti hui jaankari aapke baare mein — isay check/edit/delete kar sakte ho. Incognito ya temporary chat memory-free hoti hai, lekin trace-free nahi — company kuch waqt (30 din) ke liye ek copy safety ke liye rakhti hai. Seedha rule: Stable rules → Instructions. Badalti jaankari → Memory. Ek kaam ka poora context → Projects.

Idea 6 (Artifacts aur Writing Blocks):

Lambi chat conversation ke liye achi hai, lekin poora kaam rakhne ke liye buri hai. Claude "artifact" banata hai — document, code, diagram, ya chhoti app jo chat ke sath khulti hai. ChatGPT "writing block" aur "code block" banata hai — chat ke andar hi editable hissa (canvas ki jagah aaya). Behtar result: sirf "dashboard banao" mat kaho — poora batao kaam kya karna chahiye, kis ke liye hai.

Idea 7 (Skills aur Plugins):

Project batata hai "kya jaankari" chahiye. Skill batati hai "kaise kaam karna" hai. Skill ek packaged tareeqa hai kisi kaam ko karne ka — instructions, examples, kabhi code. Dono, Claude aur ChatGPT, Skills rakhte hain, aur "Agent Skills open standard" ki wajah se ek jagah banayi Skill doosri jagah bhi chal jati hai. Plugin ek bara package hai jo Skills + Connectors + commands sab ek sath jorta hai. ChatGPT ke "Custom GPTs" alag configured assistant hote hain, Skill se alag cheez.

Hissa 3: Rasai Barhana

Idea 8 (Connectors, Apps, aur Sahi Raasta):

Connector (Claude) ya App (ChatGPT) AI ko aapki asal app (email, calendar, Drive) tak pohanchne deta hai. MCP wo common standard hai jispar ye chalte hain — jaise "USB-C" plug, lekin plug standard hone ka matlab access safe hona nahi hai. Kisi bhi cheez connect karne se pehle 4 sawal poocho: ye kya parh sakta hai? kya likh/bhej/delete kar sakta hai? kya mujhe source par bharosa hai? kya mujhe ye connect karne ki ijazat hai?

Research mode wali baat:

Research mode "behtar search" nahi hai — AI poori investigation plan karta hai, kai searches karta hai, minutes lagte hain, aur citations ke sath report deta hai. Isay use karne ke baad bhi zimmedari khatam nahi hoti — zaroori baatein khud check karo, citations khol kar dekho.

Idea 9 (Purane Kaam Par Sabit Karo):

Sabse zaroori sawal: kaise pata chale AI aapke kaam mein waqai acha hai? Jawab: benchmark ya demo par nahi — aapke apne asal kaam par, jiska sahi jawab aapko pehle se pata ho. 5 steps: 1) baar baar hone wala kaam chuno. 2) purani misaal dhoondo jiska sahi jawab pata hai. 3) AI se wahi kaam dobara banwao. 4) apne jaane huye jawab se compare karo. 5) behtar karo, dobara chalao. Agar match ho jaye, trust mil jata hai. Agar na ho, wo bhi zaroori seekh hai — pata chal jata hai kya insaan ke paas rehna chahiye.

Zaroori baat pass hone ke baad:

Test pass hona sirf "milte julte future kaam" ke liye confidence deta hai — ye kabhi zimmedari transfer nahi karta. Aap phir bhi naye results check karte ho, final kaam ki zimmedari lete ho, aur zaroorat par batate ho ke AI ne madad ki.

End Summary (Aik Nazar Mein)

  • Claude aur ChatGPT alag naam use karte hain lekin asal discipline wahi hai — ek seekho, dono jagah lagao
  • Model chunna: Fast (rozmarra), Thinking (mushkil sawal), Heavy (sabse mushkil) — naam badalte hain, ye 3 levels hamesha rehte hain
  • AI sirf wo dekh sakta hai jo aap saamne rakho — files/context dena sabse zaroori aadat hai
  • Project = ek kaam ka poora kamra (chats + Knowledge + Instructions). Test: 3 baar wahi cheez dobara batayi to Project banao
  • Stable rule = Instructions. Badalti jaankari = Memory. Ek kaam ka context = Project
  • Artifacts/Writing blocks poore kaam ko chat scroll se bahar rakhte hain
  • Project batata hai "kya jaankari", Skill batati hai "kaise kaam" — dono alag cheezein hain
  • Connector/App aapki asal apps tak reach deta hai — connect karne se pehle 4 sawal poocho
  • Sahi raasta chuno: current fact = search, mushkil soch = thinking, poori report = research
  • AI ko purane, jaane huye kaam par test karo — yehi asal trust kamane ka tareeqa hai
